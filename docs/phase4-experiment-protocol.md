The Cooja harness then removes the whole mote at its DEPLETED event; REMOVED is retained. This simulator cutoff prevents subsequent routing timers from restoring forwarding. No new grant/replacement is allowed. Already-in-flight boundary: events at the same simulator timestamp may precede removal; transmissions finishing strictly after REMOVED fail validation. Repeat at250ms to quantify detection sensitivity. Report energy overshoot/detection resolution.

- Battery set excludes root. FND is first verified death; HND is ceil(N/2)-th death. Unobserved events are right-censored at the horizon. Functional service: 10s planned-demand cohort, accepted delivery within5s, ratio<95% for10s continuously, evaluated every1s. Empty-demand windows are not successful service observations. Record crossing/confirmation times; separate generated-reading PDR from scheduled-demand service. This engineering rule is fixed before depletion tests and must be reassessed/frozen before main research, without changing old results.
- Final phase acceptance additionally requires idle/load conservation, sample/telemetry overhead, source/config/model freeze, actual radio loss/reordering/critical flush and both-profile reset/provisioning negatives. Human/reproduction sign-off remains pending until performed.

## Emulated comparison timing contract (frozen before new four-arm Z1 groups)

Native controls retain exact global/local GEN equality. For Z1, the independent demand contract is identical sender/reading IDs and values, configured warm-up/rate/count; realized local-created timestamps and global GEN timestamps may differ by at most one declared 125ms application service tick across arms. The runner records maximum local/global jitter and the exact-equality result separately and fails the group above either bound. It never labels a bounded-jitter group exact-identical. This permits reporting real emulated CPU scheduling cost; it is an explicit engineering-protocol refinement, not retrospective repair of older failed groups or a frozen Phase6 scientific tolerance. Queue buffers are four for every Phase4 arm, source-route graph capacity includes root, logging/model/settings remain identical apart from profile/cap. Component energy figures use the declared boot-to-horizon window, including warm-up/drain; node terminal samples end their budget accounting and root is excluded from primary sums.
